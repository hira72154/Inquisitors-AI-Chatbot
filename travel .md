# Travel Assistance

The Travel Assistance section helps users plan trips and access travel-related information.

## Main Options

- Plan Trip
- Travel Tips
- Emergency

## Trip Planning

Users can enter:

- Starting city
- Destination
- Date

After entering the required information, users can search for travel options.

## Popular Destinations

The interface displays popular destinations such as:

- Karachi
- Mianwali
- Raheem Yar Khan
- Kashmir
- Muzaffarabad
- Lyari

## Emergency Travel Support

MAA provides an emergency travel support section for situations where users need urgent travel assistance.

Options include:

- Book Emergency Travel
- Talk to MAA
