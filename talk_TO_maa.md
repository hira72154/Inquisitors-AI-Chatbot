# AI Companion — Talk to MAA

The "Talk to MAA" feature provides an AI-based conversational interface.

MAA communicates with users through a chat interface and is designed to provide supportive responses.

## Suggested Prompts

Users can interact with MAA through suggested prompts such as:

- How are you, MAA?
- I'm feeling stressed
- Suggest dinner
- I miss home

## User Interaction

Users can also type their own messages in the chat input.

The AI Companion is presented as a caring digital assistant that listens, supports, and checks on users.
