# MAA Digital Companion Platform

## Project Overview

MAA is a digital companion web application designed to provide users with helpful everyday services through a single platform. The platform focuses on making daily life easier by offering home-made food services, medicine and health assistance, travel support, and an AI companion.

MAA is designed as a caring digital assistant that helps users manage different needs from one place.

## Project Purpose

The purpose of MAA is to provide users with a simple and supportive digital platform where they can access essential services and assistance.

The platform combines multiple services into one website:

- Home-made food
- Medicine and health assistance
- Travel assistance
- AI companion
- Emergency support
- User account management
