# MAA Digital Companion Platform

## Project Overview

MAA is a digital companion web application designed to provide users with helpful everyday services through a single platform. The platform focuses on making daily life easier by offering home-made food services, medicine and health assistance, travel support, and an AI companion.

MAA is designed as a caring digital assistant that helps users manage different needs from one place.

## Project Purpose

The purpose of MAA is to provide users with a simple and supportive digital platform where they can access essential services and assistance.

The platform combines multiple services into one website:

- Home-made food
- Medicine and health assistance
- Travel assistance
- AI companion
- Emergency support
- User account management

## Target Users

MAA is designed for users who need convenient access to everyday support and services. The platform can be useful for:

- Students
- Working professionals
- Senior citizens
- People living away from home
- Travelers
- Users looking for an AI-based companion

## Main Features

### User Registration

New users can create an account using:

- Name
- Email
- Phone number
- Password
- Confirm password

The registration form also includes agreement to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

### User Login

Existing users can log in to their MAA account using the login option available on the website.

### Home Page

The home page introduces the MAA platform and provides navigation to its main services.

The main navigation includes:

- Home
- Food
- Medicine
- Travel
- Talk to MAA
- Login
- Get Started

The homepage also contains user testimonials under the "Loved by Many" section.
