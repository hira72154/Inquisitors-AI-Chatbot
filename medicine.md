# Medicine & Health

The Medicine & Health section provides users with access to medicine-related services and health support.

## Main Options

- Order
- Reminders
- Health Tips

## Medicine Search

Users can search for medicines and add them to a cart.

## Example Medicines

- Paracetamol 500mg
- Vitamin D3 1000IU
- Cetirizine 10mg

The medicine section is intended to make medicine-related tasks more convenient for users.
