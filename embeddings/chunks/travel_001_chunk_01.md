# Travel Assistance

The Travel Assistance section helps users plan trips and access travel-related information.

## Main Options

- Plan Trip
- Travel Tips
- Emergency

## Trip Planning

Users can enter:

- Starting city
- Destination
- Date

After entering the required information, users can search for travel options.
