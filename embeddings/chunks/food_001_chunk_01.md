# Home-Made Food

The Home-Made Food section allows users to explore food prepared by local home cooks.

## Features

- Search for cooks, cuisines, or dishes
- Apply filters
- View available home cooks
- View cook ratings
- View preparation time
- View location
- View food categories
- Explore popular dishes
- View menus
